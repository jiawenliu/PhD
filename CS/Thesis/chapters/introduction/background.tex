People use program analysis as tools to study and control a program’s execution behavior.
A few decades ago, people employed computer programs solely for research and business purposes with the simple expectation of executing correctly without bugs. 
That is, with respect to programs, execution correctness is the most critical behavior.
As a result, the program analysis community has paid a lot of/great attention to analyzing functional correctness properties.
These properties determine whether a program executes correctly and seamlessly without bugs.
However, due to the popularity of mobile devices and the wide application of big data, the expectations on programs have increased.
It is no longer enough for these programs to only run without bugs. 
In the past couple of decades, the “non-functional” execution properties have surpassed the functional  properties in importance. 
To provide people with high-quality mobile programs, their non-functional properties must be studied. 
Therefore, we study two non-functional properties, specifically two \emph{quantitative-reachability} properties which can significantly influence people's daily life in terms of the \emph{convenience},
\emph{enjoyment}, \emph{efficiency}, and \emph{privacy}.

One of the most critical non-functional properties is the \emph{adaptivity} rounds.
This property captures the generalization error in terms of whether the sample data that is queried by the algorithm are dependent on each other (the \emph{reachability}) and how many times they are dependent (the \emph{quantitative}).
In this sense, it can be expressed from two perspectives, the \emph{quantitative} and \emph{reachability}.
We are interested in analyzing it as a \emph{quantitative-reachability} property using the principles of programming languages to help reduce the generalization error of the corresponding algorithm.
In this sense, we can help to improve the accuracy for the recommendation results computed by these algorithms
and guarantee the \emph{convenience} and the \emph{enjoyment} of people's life.
